About

VisPsych

Purpose

VisPsych is designed as a relationship-centred knowledge base: visualization design features engage psychological mechanisms, which influence cognitive or behavioural effects, tasks and outcomes.

Ontology

Visualization case → design feature → psychological mechanism → effect → task/outcome → design implication, qualified by evidence and context.

Psychology taxonomy

Concepts are organised into seven mechanism-oriented families: perception, attention, memory and learning, cognition and mental representation, judgement and decision, emotion and affect, and social influence. Semiotics, epistemology, Critical Theory, Actor–Network Theory and visual rhetoric are retained as adjacent interpretive or sociotechnical lenses rather than labelled as psychological mechanisms.

What is a relationship?

A relationship describes how a visualization design feature may engage a psychological concept, influence a perceptual, cognitive or behavioural outcome, and lead to a design implication, supported or qualified by scholarly evidence.

Status

This prototype contains illustrative seed data. Replace or extend it with reviewed evidence before treating relationship strengths or evidence status as scholarly claims.

Knowledge-model roadmap

Every relationship now has placeholders for reviewed papers, DOI, study type, participants, visualization and task, findings, evidence strength and curator notes. Once populated, the platform can add deeper nested concepts beneath the implemented expandable subcolumns, stable case URLs such as /cases/text-similarity/, and richer interactive demonstrations within each case study.
